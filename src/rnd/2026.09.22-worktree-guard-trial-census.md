## A measurement defect in the probe method

Three silent days (09-20, 09-21, 09-22) is the exact shape of a dead hook, so I probed rather than recording a zero. The probe wrote its line and the hook is alive — but **the probe as I first wrote it could not have told me that.**

I ran `count → probe → count` inside **one** Bash call. The delta read **zero** while the line was demonstrably written; only the tail proved it.

**Cause:** the guard is a `PreToolUse` hook. It fires *before* the command body, so the "before" count is taken *after* the hook has already appended.

⇒ A self-contained before/after probe of a `PreToolUse` hook is **structurally incapable of showing its own increment**, and the null result reads exactly like a dead hook.

⇒ **Correct method:** count in call A, probe in call B, count in call C. Never in one call.

⚠️ Day 6's amendment records "the log gained a line" without stating how it was counted. If it was counted the same way, that reading was luck rather than method.
