## Context

The explainer currently spends roughly two hundred lines on the fleet catching its own
instrument lying — five self-disclosed defects in the DM judge, two frontier models
disagreeing about a scoring table that measurement then killed, an in-memory counter that
erased itself on every bounce, and a corpus file that turned out to be 32 test fixtures in 34
rows.

Every one of those is honest and every one is a detour. Read end to end they make the effort
look like a series of recoveries, and the reader arrives at the two-arm experiment thinking
*"these people spent a week debugging their own thermometer"* rather than *"they were
disciplined about what they'd measured before they designed a trial."* **The design is the
achievement; the chronicle of arriving at it is currently outrunning it.**

The intended outcome: the story runs from the channel-shaped finding — plain speech to the
operator, drifting jargon between agents — to the instrument that read the channel, to the
diagnosis of why the channel does that to a speaker, and lands on the experiment now running.

## What gets written

**A new closing act, replacing lines 856–882** (*"Where this goes next"*, now stale).

Sourced entirely from the four design documents in
`lupin/src/rnd/v0.2.0/2026.08.04-dm-verbosity-reduction/`, no new claims:

- **The baseline that forced it** — 776 graded DMs, length flat across 24 hours
  (ρ=+0.036, t=+0.92), median 91 words against a 60-word target, 78% over. All in one
  condition, so *"nothing happened"* and *"nothing was detectable"* can't be separated. **That
  is the sentence that makes an experiment necessary rather than optional.**
- **The design** — two arms, blind versus rejecting at 150 words, 28 hour-blocks across two
  14-hour days, Wednesday mirroring Tuesday at every clock hour so both conditions are
  observed at every hour.
- **Why two and not three** — the window is 28 blocks, not the ~60 the first design assumed.
  Three arms leave 9–10 blocks each; two give 14 and permit a matched-clock crossover.
- **The two co-primaries** — all-attempts mean excess words *and* first-attempts-only. Both
  move together or you measured retries, not restraint.
- **What it will and won't claim** — a directional result across 14 matched pairs; not a
  dose-response curve, not a causal claim about learning.
- **What comes after** — per your ruling, the graded arm returns and the trial goes to three
  arms once the demo is behind us. Two arms is the shape the 28-block window allows this
  week, not the shape the question deserves forever.

**Also updated:**

- The instrument table (~line 939): rejection moves from `designed, unbuilt` to running, with
  its window.
- The through-line (~line 1005): one paragraph extending *"Act 12 is the point where rules ran
  out"* to *"and the instrument is the point where measurement ran out"* — the same move, one

- Sources (~line 1042): the four design documents added; the three-arm plan listed as
