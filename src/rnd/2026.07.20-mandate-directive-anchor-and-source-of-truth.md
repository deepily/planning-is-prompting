## 1. The defect

`workflow/provisional-mandates.md` requires *"One `##` block that is THE directive"* — and never says **which** block. The spawn is told to *"append its Directive block VERBATIM"* and must therefore **interpret** which of ~9 `##` blocks that is.

⇒ A mandate author renames their directive heading → the append picks the wrong block or none → **briefs ship with no mandate, silently, reporting success.**

**This is not hypothetical. It happened today.** Rick moved the demo Monday → Thursday; both live mandates were amended and the directive heading changed `## TWO DAYS` → `## THREE DAYS`.

### 1a. Verified at source, 2026-07-20

| File | sha256 (12) | Directive heading | Delivery line names the anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DATA01/google/parallel-search/MANDATE.md` | `9c9c2f89d6fd` | `## ⏱️ THREE DAYS. ONE PROCESS. END TO END.` | ``## THREE DAYS`` |
| `DATA01/google/skills-distillation/MANDATE.md` | `b46ffdd9a3e0` | `## ⏱️ THREE DAYS. ONE PROCESS. END TO END.` | ``## THREE DAYS`` |

**⭐ The anchor name is load-bearing in the mandate's OWN text.** Each file's `## Delivery` section states: *"Every spawned worker gets `## THREE DAYS` appended to its brief verbatim, by construction."* So the mandate **self-describes its own anchor** — and Rick's amendment changed that self-description in both files on the same morning.

⇒ **The anchor is a moving target that the document names in two places and the mechanism names in none.**

### 1b. A peer claim that did NOT survive checking

Rio reported: *"parallel-search's MANDATE.md is byte-identical to skills-distillation's (H1 aside)."*

**FALSE, verified**: `9c9c2f89d6fd` ≠ `b46ffdd9a3e0`. They differ at line 10 (`AFTERNOON, EAST COAST TIME` vs `EAST COAST TIME`) and line 15 (different amendment notes).

His *concern* — two near-copies drifting under independent edits — is real and is what §3 addresses. His *measurement* was not taken. **Third peer file-claim today that failed verification** (Rio's §7.1 literal, Mr Radio's `3b0f3923` origin, this one).

### 1c. What is NOT a defect — recorded so nobody re-raises it

`find / -name MANDATE.md` returns **four** files: the two above, plus a `DATA02` pair dated 07-18 still reading `## ⏱️ TWO DAYS`, `THE DEMO IS MONDAY 2026-07-20`, and `## TWO DAYS` as the append anchor.

**DATA02 is a BACKUP tree**, not a live workspace — its own HEAD commit is *"Add backup script, with .git deliberately NOT excluded"*, same root history as DATA01. A backup preserving pre-amendment state is a backup working correctly.

⚠️ **The one caveat worth stating**: rule 2 of `provisional-mandates.md` is *"the file's existence is the in-force flag; to end it, `rm MANDATE.md`."* That rule assumes **exactly one copy**. `rm` on the live path leaves the backup copy intact and still readable. This is fine **so long as nothing is ever spawned against a backup path** — which is a precondition the mechanism never states. Recorded as a **known boundary, not a defect to fix here.**

## 2. Fix — a fixed anchor the human-facing text cannot move

### 2.1 The required shape gains one rule

The directive block MUST be titled exactly:

```markdown
## DIRECTIVE
```

The human-facing framing — the day count, the deadline, the urgency — moves **inside** the block as body text or a sub-heading:

```markdown
## DIRECTIVE

### ⏱️ THREE DAYS. ONE PROCESS. END TO END.
...
```

⇒ **Renaming `TWO DAYS` → `THREE DAYS` becomes a body edit, not an anchor change.** The exact operation Rick performed this morning stops being able to break the append.

### 2.2 Why not read the Delivery line instead

The mandate already self-describes its anchor in `## Delivery`. A spawn *could* parse that line. **Rejected**: it makes the anchor a value the author must keep synchronized in two places — the same class of defect as the flag-someone-must-flip that rule 2 exists to eliminate. **A fixed anchor removes a decision; a self-describing anchor adds a synchronization obligation.**

### 2.3 Surfaces to change

| Surface | Change |
|---|---|
| `workflow/provisional-mandates.md` § required shape | `## DIRECTIVE` mandated; add to the required-properties table |
| `workflow/swe-team-spin-up.md` §7.1 | "append its Directive block" → "append the `## DIRECTIVE` block" |
| `.claude/commands/spin-up-swe-team.md` §5a | same |
| `.claude/skills/spin-up-swe-team/SKILL.md` §4a | same |

## 3. The source-of-truth question — Rio's flag

Two mandates that are near-copies will keep drifting under independent edits (§1b measured the drift: they already differ in two places).

**Recommendation: do NOT introduce a shared master.** A mandate is project-scoped **by construction** — that is rule 1, and it is the whole reason the mechanism works. A shared source-of-truth copy would reintroduce exactly the unscoped surface Rick rejected on 2026-07-16.

⇒ **What is shared is the SHAPE, not the CONTENT.** The template lives in `provisional-mandates.md` § required shape (already canonical). The per-project files stay independent and are *supposed* to diverge — different demos, different deadlines, different non-negotiables.

**What Rio's flag actually justifies** is narrower: when an edit is driven by a **fleet-level event** (Rick moves a shared demo date), it must reach every affected mandate. That is a **dispatch** problem, not a source-of-truth problem — and it is precisely what Mr Radio's spawn-brief rule addresses from the other end.

**Rio withdrew the flag as stated** on 2026-07-20 after reading this section: *"I asked for a source-of-truth copy when what I'd actually observed was uncoordinated dispatch."*

### 3a. ⚠️ THE GAP RIO FOUND IN THIS SECTION — dispatch needs a TRIGGER, not a diligent author

His push, and it holds:

> *"Dispatch-on-fleet-event still needs someone to notice the event is fleet-level. Rick told ME the date moved; nothing made me ask 'which other mandates does this touch.' Sam's copy got fixed because he was told separately, not because my edit reached him."*

⇒ **§3's dispatch framing, as first written, relied on the amending seat recognizing that its edit had fleet scope.** That is a rule in the author's head, not a mechanism — and it is **the same shape as the anchor defect itself**: correct only while every reader is a model reading prose carefully.

**Measured instance, this morning**: both mandates were amended for the same fleet event. Neither amendment reached the other. They converged because Rick told two seats separately — **the coordination was Rick's, performed twice, and nothing in the mechanism did it.**

**⇒ THIRD INSTANCE TODAY of "a rule that survives only because someone happened to do the right thing"** — after the un-parked board row (Mr Radio) and the un-renamed heading (this doc's §1). Rio's own summary of his failure is the sharpest statement of the family:
> *"A document asserting a fact about its own present tense."* — his `TODO.md:34` byte-identical line was true when Tiffany wrote it on 07-18, falsified by his own 09:xx edits, and re-quoted as current 2h later.

**Not designed here.** This section names the gap and refuses to paper it with an exhortation. Candidate directions, for Rick's ruling, none costed:

**1. CENSUS MODE — recommended, and the reasoning is Rio's.** The `/plan-mandate` pointer gains a census: *show every live `MANDATE.md`, its expiry, and its directive heading.* Then *"which others does this touch"* is one call rather than a question nobody thinks to ask.

> **Rio's argument, which decided it:** *"Declared event scope needs the amending seat to classify correctly at edit time — the same 'rule in the author's head' one step earlier, just with a field to fill in wrong. A census answers 'which others does this touch' AFTER the fact too, so a missed classification is still recoverable."*

⇒ **Declared scope fails closed-and-silent; a census fails open-and-checkable.** Same axis as WRITE-FIRST beating CHECK-THE-POINTER on the memento path (`eda57c05`): the mechanism that *removes* a decision beats the one that *adds a step*, and the one that stays **recoverable** beats both.

**2. Declared event scope** (`fleet` vs `project`) — a fleet-scoped amendment fans out by construction. **Weakened by Rio's objection above**: it does not remove the judgment, it relocates it and adds a new place to be silently wrong. Recorded because it may still be worth pairing WITH a census, not instead of one.
