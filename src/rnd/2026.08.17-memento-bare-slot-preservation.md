## Why "re-verify clean" was unreachable

The row's step 3 asked for zero findings after `migrate --apply`. The tool says otherwise, in its own
remedy text, which is the thing to read before trusting a plan built on it:

> `THIS FINDING REMAINS — migrate never removes a bare slot.`

`migrate` **preserves**: it twins the content to an immutable dated path and mirrors it out of repo.
**Clearing** a bare slot is a separate step — `write --slot io --persona <p> --session-id <sid>` —
which lands a real record and writes a true pointer over the path. Preservation and clearing are two
different operations, and only one of them was in the instructions.
