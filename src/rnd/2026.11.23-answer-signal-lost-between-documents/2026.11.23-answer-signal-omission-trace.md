# Trace: how yes/no/neither-with-comment and multiple-choice Other text were lost

María 🌸, 2026-11-23, read-only, row `69821dc6`. Lupin paths are relative to `lupin/`; planning-is-prompting paths are prefixed `pip/`. Lupin HEAD read: `99b9563f7`.

## Verdict

**No plan ever wrote the answer down as yes or no.** The expert's backlog, the remediation plan and the principle learner plan all name three stances, a comment on each, and Other text. The answer was flattened at **seams**, places where a requirement in one document had to be carried by work defined in another, with no test to carry it:

1. **The principle learner (B1) was ruled to reuse remediation phase 6's forecaster (C0)**, but phase 6 never stated what that forecaster may reply. The builder picked "yes" or "no", and B1 inherited it.
2. **The learner plan's "return stance and qualifier, write-in expected" had no acceptance test**, so nothing failed when the build could not do it.
3. **The frozen experiment froze every tunable setting except the answer space and the scoring rule.**
4. **The remediation plan's stance guard named the trust-gate file as out of scope**, and phase 12 defined trust "success" without saying how a neither or a qualified yes counts.
5. **Multiple-choice Other text was named "write-in" from the first document onwards.** That name hid "an option plus a comment" and "a re-frame" inside "a different answer".

The cascades did not catch any of this. Both reviews were organised by **section**, and each of these losses sits **between** sections or between plans.
