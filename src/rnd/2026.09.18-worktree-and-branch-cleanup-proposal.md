### Why lupin looks different from the others

lupin has a **janitor** in the arbiter (`lupin-app.ini:890`, turned on, 6-hour threshold). It removes abandoned worktrees, and that is why lupin has only 2. But:

1. **It only looks at lupin's own `.claude/worktrees`.** lupin-mobile and planning-is-prompting have no janitor.
2. **It keeps the branch, by design** (`worktree_reaper.py:695`: *"commits any WIP to the branch and KEEPS the branch"*). That keeps it safe, because a wrong reap loses nothing. But nothing ever deletes the branch afterwards. **That is the 80 branches.**
3. Worktrees made outside `.claude/worktrees` (the `pisp-wt-*` folders next to the repo, `/tmp` scratchpads) are invisible to it.

## 4. Never again — prevention

A rule that depends on remembering is not installed, so every fix below is a mechanism.

| # | Mechanism | Closes |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **The janitor deletes the branch too, once it is fully merged** into the repo's current WIP (`git branch -d`, which refuses anything unmerged). Unmerged branches are kept and reported | the 80-branch pile |
| **P2** | **The janitor covers every fleet repo** (lupin, lupin-mobile, planning-is-prompting), from a list in the ini | the 26 unwatched worktrees |
| **P3** | **Seat teardown removes its own worktree and merged branch** in `dismiss_sessions` and at session-end, **only after the seat's process is gone** (`seat_teardown.py:154` waits until there's no tmux session and no process in the tree). The janitor stays as the backstop for seats that get hard-killed. ⚠️ Never brief a LIVE seat to `git worktree remove` its own tree: deleting a running seat's cwd sent Rachel's reap looking for the memento in a directory that no longer existed (2026-09-18) | workers leaving trash |
| **P4** | **Weekly report cron**: `worktree_orphan_scan.py` across all three repos. It sends you a single card **only if** something is older than 48h or not merged | early warning, silent when clean |
| **P5** | **Creation guard (already live, trial day 5)**: new worktrees go only under `.claude/worktrees`, never next to the repo or in `/tmp` | the blind spots in §2 point 3 |
| **P6** | **Backups skip `.claude/worktrees/`** (Tiffany, measured): none of the three repos' `rsync-exclude.txt`, nor the template, excluded it. lupin-mobile's DATA02 mirror went from 794 files (09-01) to 11,589, and 112 of its 177 MB are worktrees. The template is now v1.1 | backup bloat from worktrees |

Build home: P1–P3 in lupin (`worktree_reaper.py`, the **live** janitor `src/lupin_arbiter_app/fleet_arbiter_loop.py:611` `make_worktree_janitor_fn`, and `dismiss_sessions`). ⚠️ Not `arbiter_bootstrap.py`: that is the dead rollback path (`arbiter in-process bootstrap enabled = false`), and this document first pointed there. Rachel caught it in review. The P1 merge check must be `merge-base --is-ancestor` against the repo's current branch, done explicitly, because `git branch -d` measures against the branch's **upstream** when it has one. P4 lives here (`workflow/scripts/`) with a crontab line. The workflow write-up (`workflow/worktree-lifecycle.md`) comes after the mechanism exists.
