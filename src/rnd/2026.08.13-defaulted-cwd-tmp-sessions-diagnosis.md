# What spawns a session with a defaulted cwd? Nothing does — three mechanisms, two already closed

**María 🌸, 2026-08-13.** Row `871b68dd`, recovered from a hold that had been raised twice and owned
by nobody. The question: *eight `-tmp` MCP failures since April is a rate, and a rate implies a
mechanism — what is it?*

**Answer: three mechanisms, all identified, two closed by fixes made for other reasons, and the
third is a person deliberately running Claude from a scratchpad. Nothing is defaulting a cwd.**

---

## The dataset the row asked for

Every Claude session leaves a per-cwd cache directory at
`~/.cache/claude-cli-nodejs/<slugified-cwd>/`. Five of them are rooted under `/tmp`, holding
**14 cosa-voice log files** between them. The hold's "eight since April" counted only the first row
of this table — which is exactly right for bare `/tmp`, and is why the other two mechanisms stayed
invisible.

| cwd class | cwds | cosa-voice logs | first | last |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bare `/tmp` | 1 | **8** | 2026-04-05 | **2026-07-13** |
| a git worktree created under `/tmp` (`edward-dr-migration-wt`) | 1 | 2 | 2026-06-17 | 2026-06-17 |
| inside a session's own scratchpad subdirectory | 3 | 4 | 2026-07-03 | 2026-07-03 |

## Mechanism 1 — bare `/tmp`: the documentation was the mechanism

Eight incidents: 04-05, 04-26, 06-01, 06-02, 06-15, 06-17, 06-30, **07-13**.

The global config used to instruct the reader to `cd /tmp && claude mcp get cosa-voice` when
diagnosing a missing MCP server. **The doc prescribed the failure**, which is why it recurred at a
steady rate across three months and across different people — each incident is someone following
instructions.

That line was removed on 2026-07-13. **The last bare-`/tmp` session is 2026-07-13 20:46**, the same
day. **Zero in the month since**, across a fleet that has been busy — the strongest available
evidence that this route is closed and that it was the dominant one.

## Mechanism 2 — a worktree under `/tmp`: closed two days after it bit

One session, 2026-06-17 23:12, launched at a git worktree that had been created under `/tmp`.
Worktree-aware project detection landed in `53fef419` on **2026-06-19** — two days later. A worktree
now resolves to its main repo's basename, and a *dangling* worktree parses its gitlink statically
rather than degrading to its own directory name.

**This incident predates its fix and has not recurred.**

## Mechanism 3 — a scratchpad subdirectory: not a defect, a repro

Three cwds, all on 2026-07-03 between 02:15 and 03:04, all under one session's scratchpad:
`.../scratchpad/wedge-repro`, `.../wedge-repro-hang`, `.../timeout-probe`. One of them also carries
an `mcp-logs-hangmcp` directory — a deliberately-hanging test MCP server.

This is an investigator reproducing a wedge, launching Claude from the directory holding the repro.
The scratchpad lives under `/tmp` and therefore has no `.git` ancestor. **The behaviour is correct
and the cwd was chosen on purpose**; what is missing is that nothing warned them the choice would
cost them their project identity.

---

## What this means for the row

The row asked whether this was *"one recurring mechanism or eight unrelated accidents"* and said the
answer decides whether it is a bug or a training issue. **It is neither shape**: one recurring
mechanism that was documentation (closed), one real defect (closed), and one deliberate act by
someone who knew where they were standing.

**Nothing spawns a session with a defaulted cwd.** No spawn path inherits a stale cwd, no
tmux/cron invocation is missing a working directory. Those candidate mechanisms are refuted by the
data rather than left open.

**The one thing still worth doing, and it is small**: mechanism 3 will happen again, because
scratchpads are under `/tmp` by design and reproducing a bug from the scratchpad is a reasonable
thing to do. A session whose cwd has no `.git` ancestor could say so once, plainly, instead of
firing an urgent validation failure on every connection. That is a message-quality change, not a
detection change — the detection is right.

**Falsifiable claim**: if any of the three mechanisms is still live, a new `-tmp*` directory will
appear under `~/.cache/claude-cli-nodejs/`. None has since 2026-07-13.
